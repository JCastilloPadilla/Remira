# Propuesta de identidad: segunda mirada

Símbolo generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes (`imagegen`), como propuesta para el detector de correos. La forma representa un mensaje plegado con una abertura diagonal: una segunda lectura de su contenido. Se utiliza el azul de la interfaz y fondo transparente, sin texto ni escudos. No se ha realizado una búsqueda de similitud de marcas.

Archivo: `frontend/public/brand/logo-segunda-mirada-v1.png`. Es un PNG rasterizado, no un archivo vectorial. El logo está incorporado en los encabezados de las siete pantallas mediante `BrandMark`, y como favicon e icono de acceso directo. Conserva el archivo original con transparencia. Los iconos que expresan clasificación y permisos mantienen su significado. La revisión visual en tamaños pequeños sigue pendiente; los detalles del pliegue pueden necesitar simplificación en una versión vectorial.

## Prompt inicial

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original, refined brand symbol for an academic web application that reviews emails for potential phishing, whose central idea is 'a second look at a message'. Asset type: standalone app icon/logo mark, square canvas, true transparent background. Design an intentionally crafted folded-message glyph: a compact, continuous thick cobalt-blue ribbon with softened geometric corners, a subtle diagonal paper fold, and two offset planes enclosing a generous negative-space reading slit. The negative space and a small purposeful upper-right cut should be its distinctive signature. Suggest correspondence and a second reading abstractly, without drawing a literal envelope or eye. Strong unified silhouette, optically balanced, quiet character, memorable proportions, highly legible at 24px, generous transparent margin roughly 18 percent. Flat solid vector-like shapes, crisp immaculate edges, single color #2457C5, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D. No words, letters, numbers, captions, mockup, border, background tile, watermark, shield, checkmark, padlock, magnifying glass, robot, circuit motif, Gmail M shape, Microsoft Outlook shape or other existing brand reference. Do not present a sheet of options: render exactly one finished logo mark, centered. Aim for a custom editorial symbol with enough simplicity to reproduce in monochrome.

## Prompt de refinamiento

Refine this generated original logo mark for production use. Preserve its recognizable folded-message silhouette, large diagonal negative-space aperture, asymmetric opening at the upper right, and cobalt blue identity. Change ONLY the rendering quality: perfectly clean mathematical-looking contours, remove stray blue fragments and speckles around edges and inside gaps, remove all tonal variation, grain, glow and shading. Every opaque interior pixel must look like the same solid cobalt #2457C5. Simplify the lower-left fold seam to a smooth clean curve; maintain generous open gaps and a strong silhouette for a 24px app icon. Flat vector-like graphic with softly rounded corners, no text, no new shapes, no mockup, no border, no background tile. True transparent background. One centered mark on a square canvas with generous margin.
